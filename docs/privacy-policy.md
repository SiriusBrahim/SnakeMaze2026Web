# Privacy Policy — Snake Maze 2026 Web

Snake Maze 2026 Web is an ad-free browser game. It does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics.

## Data stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress and preferences, including scores, career statistics, achievements, settings, and custom mazes, in your browser's local storage. This data is not sent to the game developer. Clearing the browser's site data removes it. After the first visit, the service worker may cache the game files on your device so the game can work offline.

## Hosting

When you visit the game, the hosting provider may process standard connection information (such as your IP address and requested files) to deliver and secure the site. See the hosting provider's privacy policy for details.
